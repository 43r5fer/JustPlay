GET CONNECTED IN A MOMENT

Your people are one code away.

Make an account, share your friend code, and let Clines handle the rest.

STEP 01 / JOIN

Create your account

Choose a username, email and password. Confirm your password and complete any email confirmation requested by Supabase.

STEP 02 / CONNECT

Share your friend code

Clines gives your profile a unique 10-digit code. Friends can use it to find you and send a request.

STEP 03 / TALK

Start your conversation

Chat one-to-one or make a group. Your messages and profile sync through your Clines account.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Cloud synced. Permission aware.

Accounts and cloud data

Clines uses Supabase Auth for email/password login, Postgres for profiles and messages, Realtime for updates, and a private Storage bucket for media. A public publishable key is used by clients; database policies enforce user access.

Supabase Auth documentation ↗

Media and privacy

Voice and video recording requires microphone/camera permission. Media is uploaded to a private bucket and accessed through temporary signed URLs. Do not share content you do not have permission to distribute.

Storage access control ↗

Open-source project

The Windows app and Android build share the same web UI. Electron packages it for PC, while Capacitor wraps it as an Android app.

Source repository ↗

Early release

This is an actively developing messenger. Verify email confirmation, friend flow, media recording, group roles and read receipts on your own devices before using it for important communication.
